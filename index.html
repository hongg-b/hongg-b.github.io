NeurIPS 2026Conference · Accepted

LLMs As MDP Designers: A Dependency-Aware Agentic Framework for Automated Robot Policy Generation

H. Je-Gal, J. Kim, C. Yi, and H.-S. Lee

Project pagearXivPDFLab record
Figure & one-minute summary

Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026

A robot simulator can run without errors even when its reward teaches the wrong behavior.

ARPG takes a written task and builds the reinforcement learning problem in stages. Its agents record the objective and constraints before defining what the robot observes, which actions it can take, and how it earns reward. A checker traces inconsistencies back to the relevant stage. Separate agents then implement the simulator and train a policy in Isaac Lab.

ResultIn 10 independent attempts per task, ARPG produced a successful policy 9 times for Ant and 9 for Franka Reach. For Franka Lift, the simulator ran in 9 attempts and only 4 yielded a successful lifting policy.

Factorized Scheduling Principle: Learning Interpretable and Transferable Policies via Structured Additive Functions

Hong Je-Gal and Hyun-Suk Lee

arXivOpenReviewPDF
Figure & one-minute summary

International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026

Which item should a scheduler serve next when the number of candidates keeps changing?

FSP learns one priority rule from scheduling experience. It represents the current candidates by the distribution of their features and scores each item by adding learned effects for individual features and pairs of features. The resulting curves show what raised or lowered an item's priority. The same rule can rank a different number of candidates without retraining.

ResultThe authors tested wireless scheduling, inventory replenishment, and warehouse clearance. In warehouse clearance, a rule trained on 10 items earned the highest average reward among the tested methods on new populations of 5, 10, 15, and 20 items.

A-LAMP: Agentic LLM-Based Framework for Automated MDP Modeling and Policy Generation

Hong Je-Gal, Chan-Bin Yi, and Hyun-Suk Lee

arXivPDF
Figure & one-minute summary

NeurIPS Workshop on Multi-Turn Interactions in Large Language Models, 2025

Can a written task specification become a working learning environment and a trained policy?

A-LAMP divides the work among language-model agents. They identify the quantities, constraints, and objective in the description, then define the state, actions, and rewards. Coding agents turn that formulation into a Gym-style environment and training loop. A code executor runs it and returns errors for repair. The evaluation scores the formulation, executable code, and learned behavior separately.

ResultIn 20 drone-delivery trials, A-LAMP with GPT-4o produced a successful policy 9 times, compared with 2 for GPT-4o alone. In wireless resource allocation the corresponding counts were 9 and 4.

A Novel Data-Driven Soft Sensor in Metaverse Provisioning Predictive Credibility Based on Uncertainty Quantification

S.-H. Park, H. Je-Gal, and H.-S. Lee

PaperScholar
Figure & one-minute summary

IEEE MetaCom · Meta-XP Workshop, pp. 285–290, 2024

A virtual air-quality sensor estimates ultrafine dust from other air-quality and weather measurements. How can it signal that a reading is uncertain?

The researchers trained five neural networks on atmospheric measurements from Seoul. Each network estimates both a mean and a variance. Together they produce a dust estimate and an interval around it. The experiment introduced an artificial shift in sensor data, either only at test time or during both training and testing.

ResultWhen the shift first appeared at test time, the intervals were about 12 times wider on average than when training had included the shift. Those wider intervals contained 95.64% of the measured test values.

JMSE 2024International journal

Time-Series Explanatory Fault Prediction Framework for Marine Main Engine Using Explainable Artificial Intelligence

H. Je-Gal, Y.-S. Park, S.-H. Park, J.-U. Kim, J.-H. Yang, S. Kim, and H.-S. Lee

Paper
Figure & one-minute summary

Journal of Marine Science and Engineering, 12(8), 1296, 2024

When a model predicts an engine fault, which sensor readings prompted the warning, and when did they occur?

Using operating data from an LNG carrier, the authors select relevant sensor channels with correlation and SHAP analysis. An LSTM forecasts cylinder exhaust-gas temperature. A learned mask then highlights the sensors and time intervals that influenced the forecast. To check those explanations against known precursors, the authors add 11 synthetic fault patterns to normal operating data.

ResultAcross the 11 datasets, the mean overlap score was 0.76. It measures the fraction of the time flagged as important for a sensor that fell within its injected precursor period.

Efficient Federated Digital Twin Synchronization in Edge-Cloud Collaborative System

J.-W. Kim, H. Je-Gal, and H.-S. Lee

PaperScholar
Figure & one-minute summary

IEEE MetaCom · Meta-XP Workshop, pp. 714–719, 2023

Where should each digital twin hand its computation from an edge device to the cloud?

A twin can finish inference at an early exit or send an intermediate result to the cloud. The best handoff depends on how often each exit is used, how long each layer takes, and how much data must cross the link. The authors compare a joint search over all twins' split points with a cheaper heuristic that chooses a split for each twin separately.

ResultIn simulations using an early-exit ResNet on CIFAR-10, the heuristic came close to the exhaustive search's synchronization time. When transmission became costly, sending all inputs to the cloud was the slowest option tested.

JMSE 2023International journal

A Novel Time–Frequency Feature Fusion Approach for Robust Fault Detection in a Marine Main Engine

H. Je-Gal, S.-J. Lee, J.-H. Yoon, H.-S. Lee, J.-H. Yang, and S. Kim

Paper
Figure & one-minute summary

Journal of Marine Science and Engineering, 11(8), 1577, 2023

Can a single shaft encoder reveal a cylinder misfire when engine speed is sampled less frequently?

During a sea trial on a crude oil carrier, the researchers deliberately induced a cylinder misfire and recorded instantaneous engine speed. They combined time-domain features of the speed signal with features from its bispectrum, which captures relationships between frequency components. They then reduced the sampling rate to test which fault signatures survived.

ResultAt one hundredth of the original sampling rate, the fused features classified normal and abnormal operation with 96.26% accuracy. Using the time-domain features alone gave 53.48% on the same test.

J-KICS 2023Domestic journal

Digital Role-Model Twin Using Reinforcement Learning Model

J.-W. Kim, H. Je-Gal, S.-J. Lee, and H.-S. Lee

Paper
Figure & one-minute summary

The Journal of Korean Institute of Communications and Information Sciences, 48(2), 282–293, 2023

Can a recommender learn how one person's choices change with the situation?

The digital role-model twin learns from the choices a role model makes among a set of options. A deep Q-network uses a learned representation of that role model together with contextual information to score the options. In a simulated news service, virtual role models had different interests and became more interested in weather news when it rained or snowed.

ResultIn rain or snow, the context-aware twin scored 99.18% on the paper's modified recall measure for news recommendations. The version that omitted context scored 90.31% in the same simulation.
