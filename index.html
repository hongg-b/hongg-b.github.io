Hong Je-Gal 제갈홍

Ph.D. Student · Sejong University

I am a Ph.D. student at Sejong University and a member of the Machine Intelligence and Networking (MAIN) Lab, working with Prof. Hyun-Suk Lee.

My research focuses on intelligent decision-making and explainable machine learning. My recent work explores agentic LLMs for automated MDP modeling and robot policy generation, as well as interpretable and transferable scheduling policies. I have also worked on explainable fault prediction and digital twins.
